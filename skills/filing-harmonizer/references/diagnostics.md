# Diagnostics

Use this guide after the panel has been built or updated.

## Core Aggregate Checks

At minimum, inspect annual series for:
- number of firms,
- aggregate sales,
- aggregate materials,
- aggregate wage bill,
- aggregate capital.

Add other variables that matter for the current build, such as taxes, exports, energy, or services.

## Plotting Priorities

1. Plot levels over time.
2. Plot growth rates or log changes around known regime breaks.
3. Plot variable ratios when scale shifts alone are not informative.

Useful ratios often include:
- materials to sales,
- wage bill to sales,
- capital to sales,
- taxes to income or taxes to value added,
- exporter share when export detail exists.

## Questions to Ask

- Does the total number of firms move in a way consistent with the source coverage?
- Do aggregate series show structural breaks exactly when the filing regime changes?
- Are breaks broad-based or driven by a small number of firms?
- Does a concept shift explain the break better than a coding error?
- Are apparent improvements just the result of adding informative rows or subtotals?

## Concentration Checks

When an aggregate series jumps sharply:
- compare extensive-margin and intensive-margin changes,
- inspect the top firms around the break,
- check whether the shift comes from newly mapped accounts,
- separate genuine coverage expansion from remapping artifacts.

## Minimum Deliverables

Save durable outputs such as:
- aggregate time-series tables,
- plots by core variable,
- a short break note for each major discontinuity,
- a ranked list of unmapped or newly added accounts by aggregate value when relevant.
